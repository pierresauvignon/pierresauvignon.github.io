the short version

I started my first business in France straight out of high school. In Sydney I was Head of Product at Pollenizer, building the product team that launched 100+ startups and sold one to Yahoo!7 for AU$40M. In Perth I grew Heyscape from an idea to a profitable business with 25 off-grid cabins. Now I build AI products on my own and lead AI projects on contract, most recently at Respondent.io, where I built an autonomous AI agent that runs their recruitment campaigns.
